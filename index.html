STUDENT BIO

As a fourth-year Information Technology student, my academic path started with the nuts and bolts of computing: relational databases, software logic, networking principles, and computer architecture. While I appreciated understanding what happens behind the screen, everything clicked into place the moment I touched interface design.

I discovered that good design is never just decoration—it is intuitive structure, intentional hierarchy, and human empathy. Bridging the gap between the logic of programming and the emotional clarity of design has become my core passion.

Completing my graduating capstone while refining a portfolio of clean, responsive web prototypes ready for internship and entry-level design positions.
