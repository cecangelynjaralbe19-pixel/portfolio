## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the personality of an emerging UI/UX designer and web technologist: thoughtful, articulate, creative, and technically grounded. It merges a contemporary digital product aesthetic with editorial warmth, avoiding sterile minimalism while maintaining rigorous clarity.

The visual direction pairs structured geometry with tactile, inviting tones:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, approachable, polished, and quietly confident.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Editorial Minimalism accented by subtle layered depth, luminous violet focal points, and warm stone-tinted containers.
- **Target Audience:** Design leads, engineering hiring managers, creative directors, and industry peers seeking modern craft and end-to-end interface proficiency.
- **Core Tenets:** High typographic legibility, clean component boundaries, accessible contrast, and refined interactive affordances optimized for lightweight, responsive web hosting.

## Colors

The palette balances a focused digital primary with warm natural neutrals to create an approachable yet elevated creative environment.

### Roles and Usage
- **Primary (`#5B44E0`):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, selected interactive states, navigation indicators, and key thematic accents.
- **Secondary (`#8B7AE8`):** Serves as an atmospheric tint, supporting hover states, delicate glows, and tag highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#E07A5F`):** A warm terracotta used sparingly for celebratory tags, process callouts, and specialized badges (such as project status or awards).
- **Neutral Core (`#201E24`):** A deep violet-tinted charcoal applied to primary headings and high-contrast typography, avoiding harsh pure black.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF8F5` (warm alabaster).
  - Surface cards: `#FFFFFF` (pure white for elevated cards and modals).
  - Subtle containers: `#F3EFEA` (warm stone for code snippets, secondary pills, and recessed zones).
  - Borders: `#E8E2D9` (soft stone outline offering structural definition without visual weight).

## Typography

The type scale combines **Outfit** for structural headings with **Manrope** for analytical prose and interface controls.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Geometric, contemporary, and confident. Generous x-height and circular forms provide an engaging editorial title aesthetic.
- **Body & Labels (Manrope):** Clean, highly legible, modern sans-serif with open apertures and balanced proportions suited for detailed case studies, design rationales, and metadata.
- **Hierarchy Rules:**
  - Case study titles and hero banners use negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to tighten letter forms at large sizes.
  - Body copy retains neutral tracking and generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) for reading comfort across extended narratives.
  - Small labels and role chips apply subtle uppercase capitalization with relaxed tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to optimize scanning speed.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular baseline coupled with a responsive 12-column grid to support varied content densities ranging from visual galleries to case study breakdowns.

### Form Factors & Adaptability
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of `1200px`, centered with `margin` of `3rem`. Standard 12-column layout with `gutter` of `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `gutter` of `1rem`, page margins set to `2rem`. Project cards collapse to 2-column configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-sm` of `1rem`. Grids collapse into vertical single-column stacks.

### Spacing Hierarchy
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline badges, button paddings, and metadata pairings.
- Use `space-md` for standard component internals (card padding, form control spacing).
- Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` for sectional spacing within modules and card-to-card separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient shadows combined with soft structural boundaries to maintain an airy, tactile presence without heavy skeuomorphism.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Recessed stone panels (`#F3EFEA`) with no shadow, demarcated by clean boundary lines (`#E8E2D9`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`) with a dual-layer soft shadow tinted with neutral charcoal and warm violet:
  - `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(32, 30, 36, 0.02), 0 8px 16px rgba(91, 68, 224, 0.04);`
  - Paired with a 1px border (`#E8E2D9`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** Lifted cards and interactive cards:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 8px rgba(32, 30, 36, 0.03), 0 16px 32px rgba(91, 68, 224, 0.08);`
  - Subtle upward translation of `-2px` on hover.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Persistent header bars and modal sheets:
  - Backdrop blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 85% opacity over the base stone palette, paired with `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px rgba(32, 30, 36, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry that complements the friendly, creative tone:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, buttons, skill tags, metadata badges, and small previews.
- **Card Radius (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Applied to project showcases, bio feature blocks, and code panels.
- **Container Radius (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Applied to modal cards, full-width feature sections, and preview frames.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Applied strictly to status tags, category filters, and floating quick-action pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#5B44E0`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`. Padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Smooth transition for background shift to `#4A35C5` and elevation to Level 2 on hover.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #E8E2D9`, text `#201E24`. Hover state shifts border to `#5B44E0` and background to tinted stone `#F3EFEA`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#5B44E0`, transparent background, subtle underline or chevron transform on hover.

### Badges & Role Chips
- **Skill Pill:** Background `#F3EFEA`, border `1px solid #E8E2D9`, text `#201E24`, font `label-sm`. Rounded to `9999px` with padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Role/Accent Badge:** Background `rgba(91, 68, 224, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(91, 68, 224, 0.2)`, text `#5B44E0`, font `label-sm`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Project Card:** Pure white background, `1rem` radius, `1px solid #E8E2D9`, padding `1.5rem`. Houses a visual mockup frame with a subtle inner border, title (`headline-sm`), brief description (`body-md`), and a footer containing contextual skill tags and view-case-study affordance. Smooth lift transition on hover.
- **Metric / Stat Card:** Background `#F3EFEA`, border `1px solid transparent`, containing large stat text (`headline-md` in `#5B44E0`) with muted caption label (`label-md`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Input & Textarea:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E8E2D9`, text `#201E24`, placeholder color `#8C8794`. Border transitions to `#5B44E0` on focus with an ambient ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 68, 224, 0.15)`. Radius set to `0.5rem`.

### Navigation Header
- Floating or sticky bar with frosted background (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bordered bottom with `#E8E2D9`. Features streamlined logo mark, inline page links (`body-sm`, bold on active), and quick link to résumé or contact CTA.
